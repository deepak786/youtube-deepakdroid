# Summary

## 📚 Traditional RAG

```text
Documents
      │
      ▼
Chunking
      │
      ▼
Embeddings
      │
      ▼
Vector Database
      │
      ▼
LLM
```

RAG is excellent at finding **similar information**.

---

## 🧠 Google's OKF

```text
Knowledge
      │
      ▼
Curated Concepts
      │
      ▼
Metadata + Links
      │
      ▼
Portable Bundle
      │
      ▼
Agent / RAG / LLM
```

OKF is an open format for representing knowledge as linked markdown concepts.

Not a database.

Not a retrieval engine.

---

## 💡 The Key Difference

**RAG asks:**

> Which documents are most similar to my question?

**OKF asks:**

> How should this knowledge be structured, linked, and shared?

---

## 🎯 Final Thoughts

RAG isn't broken.

It's still one of the most effective ways to build AI applications today.

But retrieving similar documents isn't always enough.

Sometimes you also need knowledge that is curated, linked, and portable.

That's the problem Google's Open Knowledge Format is trying to address.

OKF is still early.

Whether it becomes widely adopted remains to be seen.

The spec is in the description.

> **For AI, organizing knowledge may become just as important as retrieving it.**
